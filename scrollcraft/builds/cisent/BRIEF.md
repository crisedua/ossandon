## Grammar

**Chaptered editorial.** The site is a credibility argument (own models, a
published-model count, four founders with long CVs), which is long-form
substance, not a product film. Why the other seven lost:

- Filmic one-shot: no footage, and a film hides the facts this brand sells on.
- Live surface: the honest pitch is "trust us with your security", not "watch
  the dashboard"; one operable figure covers the demo without making the whole
  page an app.
- Continuous world: no geography to travel.
- Typographic poster: there are real faces and real data to show.
- Gallery: three products are not a range to browse.
- Split stage: no two-sided argument in the copy.
- Rhythmic cutlist: wrong energy for security buyers.

Nav: a folio in the left margin (wordmark, chapter, the incident ledger, the CTA
as a text link). Hero: a title page, type on paper, no media. Close: a colophon.

## Signature move

**The incident runs in the margin.** The folio carries a quiet ledger
("Watching") for the whole page. In the Defense chapter the visitor's scroll is
the clock: events arrive, the brute force is detected, the folio stripe turns
vermilion, the address is blocked, the stripe settles. The ledger keeps the
record. In the Report chapter the same events, computed from the same data
array, come back typeset as an incident report the visitor can download.

## Feeling curve

```
1  Composure     a title page in plain type on paper, no glow, nothing moving
2  Respect       "We don't integrate AI. We build it." then 4 of 15 ticks into a tally
3  Recognition   the phishing screenshot, the photographed dashboard, their own week
4  Hush          a full-stop intertitle, "II  Defense", and nothing else (authored silence)
5  Tension>relief the attack arrives in the margin and the figure, is blocked, settles
6  Edge          a hard cut to an ink ground: Red Team, attack yourself first
7  Order         paper again, the incident from act 5 filed as a report
8  Trust         four real faces, plain CVs, no cards
9  Resolve       one question, one line of running text, done
```
